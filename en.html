Prof. Dr. Ünsal Ban

An academic whose published work includes finance, banking profitability, financial distress prediction and capital-market research. The AVESİS academic record lists him as Rector of the Turkish Aeronautical Association University from 2011 to 2015.

Academic administration

AVESİS lists a 2011–2015 rectorship at the Turkish Aeronautical Association University.

Banking research

A 2010 paper indexed by RePEc examines internal determinants of profitability in the Turkish banking sector.

Corporate finance

Google Books lists Değere Dayalı İşletme Finansı: Finansal Yönetim, co-authored with Metin Kamil Ercan.
